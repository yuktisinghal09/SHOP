Our Heritage & Mission

Heeralal Pannalal Saree Emporium

Located at Sethbada, Holi Gate in the sacred and historic city of Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, Heeralal Pannalal Saree Emporium is a specialized boutique dedicated to women's ethnic and traditional Indian attire.

Managed and curated by proprietor Amit Singhal, our emporium is committed to presenting authentic traditional clothing that celebrates India's legendary textile art. We offer an extensive selection of sarees, bridal lehengas, Anarkali suits, sharara sets, gharara sets, dupattas, and festive wear designed for weddings, cultural festivals, pujas, and special milestones.

Every ensemble in our boutique is chosen for its fabric purity, weave clarity, intricate embroidery, and rich silhouettes. From the grandeur of royal Banarasi silk drapes to lightweight festive georgettes, our collections ensure that every woman feels graceful and radiant on her special day.
